# Implementation design

Assessment is authored before dialogue. Each question or commitment has visible effects and private weights for leadership, people and decision evidence. Information-seeking provides provisional evidence. At commitment, positive evidence is weighted by recorded information confidence; negative choices remain separately recorded. A stock-policy mistake with explicit policy information is distinguished from an unsupported assumption. This does not establish what a candidate privately understood.

All major decisions remain on one 15-decision timeline. Information branches, two optional coworker exchanges and the conditional appeal reconnect. The live mentor selects the conditions at two checkpoints. All nonhuman dialogue is deterministic. Different hires change work duration, review needs and task ownership; business authority remains with the client sponsor and Alex's approvals.

The two savings variants are favorable-condition outcomes, not certificates of management quality. Ending categorization independently evaluates delivery evidence and management history. Bonus eligibility uses specific recorded facts and commitments; human mentor free text is not automatically evaluated.

Private evidence is removed at the server's player view boundary. Player exports use only that view. Role tokens are private capabilities and should not be shared outside the intended pair. SQLite stores project state, checkpoints, feedback and completed paths. Browser storage keeps role links, reading progress and unsaved note drafts; committed game state remains server-owned.

Design areas still needing evaluation: calibration of assessment weights, task estimates, breadth of role-specific prose, interpersonal reaction nuance, reference-route fairness, session length and real-browser visual behavior. Exact monetary targets are authored examples, not estimates of a real deployment.

## V1.2 discovery changes

The persisted schema is version 3. Read acknowledgements are protagonist-only actions and track messages displayed in an active chat. Introductory messages must be read before the corresponding options unlock. Authored fact prerequisites control knowledge-dependent commitments. Answer facts enter the brief on read, not merely when a question is selected. A post-commit read does not backfill the historical decision's facts.

Candidate profiles are an alternate information source. Opening them records the capacity information at the current decision, never retroactively at hiring. Internal information-coverage weights describe the information available, not comprehension or intent. Fixed dialogue cannot reliably distinguish deliberate misrepresentation, misunderstanding or missing clarification.

All information questions live in their owner's DM. Main commitments retain their authored group/DM setting. Green unread indicators and continuation links identify actual story movement; amber indicators identify available optional conversations. The latter expire at commitment. Known facts and message history persist.

The deadline clock forecasts a reference completion workload from current staffing and agreed conditions. Green requires at least fourteen calendar days of projected margin; yellow indicates zero to thirteen days; red indicates negative projected margin or an overdue accepted deadline. Forecasts do not include future mentor constraints. Completed delivery uses its accepted-date result rather than predicting further work.

The owner-only Branch_Map.html uses executed engine routes for outcome colors. Its 51 commitment branches are distinct (decision, key) pairs, not complete paths. The diagram is intentionally separate from the player UI because it contains spoilers and private branch requirements.
